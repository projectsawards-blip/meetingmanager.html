🔧 Availability Adjustment Process:

Mark off availability for anyone who submits an email request using these precise steps:

  1. Go to Forum Settings > Availability Page Settings > enable availability page.
  2. Go to Content Settings > Manage Person > search the person's name.
  3. Copy their unique login link and open it in a Chrome Incognito window.
  4. Click the Availability Tab and select the meeting slots they cannot make.
  5. Return to your main window: Forum Settings > Availability Page Settings > disable availability page.
Recommendation: Track all requested availability updates manually in your master Excel tracker file as well.

📋 Role Profile Reference

Job Purpose

The Meetings Manager is responsible for planning, coordinating and delivering a high-quality pre-scheduled meetings programme for buyers and suppliers at business events. This role ensures the meetings platform is accurately configured, participants are supported throughout the process, and meeting schedules are optimised to maximise networking opportunities and event value.

Key Responsibilities
• Meetings Programme Management
  • Build and configure the meetings platform for each event.
  • Set up supplier companies, meeting packages and appointment schedules.
  • Manage buyer and supplier registration within the meetings platform.
  • Configure meeting times, table allocations and scheduling parameters.
  • Maintain accurate participant data throughout the project lifecycle.
• Participant Management
  • Communicate key deadlines and provide guidance to buyers and suppliers.
  • Monitor registrations and chase outstanding actions, including profile completion and wishlist selections.
  • Record participant availability and any special scheduling requirements.
  • Respond to participant queries in a timely and professional manner.
• Meeting Scheduling
  • Run the meeting matching process using the event platform.
  • Review schedules to ensure the best possible match quality.
  • Identify and resolve duplicate or conflicting meetings.
  • Publish multiple schedule iterations and manage amendments.
  • Liaise with internal sales teams to optimise buyer meeting volumes where required.
  • Produce final meeting schedules and table allocations ahead of the event.
• Event Delivery
  • Print and prepare meeting schedules and table plans.
  • Manage the meetings programme onsite, including ad hoc meeting requests.
  • Update the meetings system with any onsite changes.
  • Record completed meetings and attendance.
  • Report on meeting completion and participant engagement following the event.
• Stakeholder Management
  • Work closely with Sales, Operations, Delegate Management and Customer Success teams.
  • Build strong relationships with buyers, suppliers and sponsors.
  • Ensure a consistently high level of customer service throughout the meetings process.
Skills & Experience
  • Experience managing hosted buyer or pre-scheduled meetings programmes.
  • Strong organisational skills with excellent attention to detail.
  • Ability to manage multiple deadlines across several events simultaneously.
  • Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
  • Strong problem-solving and stakeholder management abilities.
  • Confident working with CRM systems and event technology platforms.
  • Advanced Microsoft Excel skills and good working knowledge of Microsoft Office.
Personal Attributes
  • Highly organised and proactive.
  • Calm under pressure with excellent time management.
  • Customer-focused with a collaborative approach.
  • Analytical with strong attention to detail.
  • Flexible and adaptable in a fast-paced events environment.
  • Logical and extremely comfortable with digital platforms.
  • Confident working with senior managers in high pressured environments.
Key Performance Indicators
  • Meetings platform launched on time.
  • Registration and profile completion targets achieved.
  • High wish list completion rates.
  • Meeting schedules published to agreed deadlines.
  • Low scheduling error and duplicate rates.
  • High participant satisfaction with meeting quality.
  • Accurate onsite attendance reporting and post-event meeting statistics.
